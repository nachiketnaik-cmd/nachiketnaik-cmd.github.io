Chapter 02 Jul 2026

Stealth startup

Founder's Office Intern

Ran outreach for an event the founder was hosting. The founder's most valuable network sat in a stack of business cards collected at conferences over the years, so I turned it into a usable contact database: digitised 300+ cards with Gemini OCR, then verified each record by hand so the list could be trusted. That database became the backbone of the event's outreach: over two weeks I sent personalised messages on the founder's behalf, with context from the founder, to 15–20 people a day, roughly 200 in all. About 80 people attended the event. I also built the Excel CRM that tracked every lead and follow-up, and helped design the event poster.
